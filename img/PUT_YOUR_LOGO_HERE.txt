logo.png in this folder is the BrewGenge crest.

It is shown:
  - large behind the login screen
  - as a subtle watermark behind the app
  - as the small round crest in the sidebar

Its background has been made transparent so it sits correctly on both the
light and dark themes.

To swap it, replace logo.png (or add logo.jpeg / logo.jpg).
Lowercase filenames only - GitHub Pages is case-sensitive.
A transparent PNG works best. If no file is found, a built-in drawn crest
is used instead, so nothing is ever a broken image.
